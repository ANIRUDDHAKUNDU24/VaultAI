Terms of Service

This VaultAI workspace is a research demonstration. Registration creates a browser-local demo account; it does not create a production account. Do not use it for operational financial decisions, customer screening, law enforcement, or regulatory determinations.

Use only synthetic information. The demo credential is public, and browser storage can be inspected or changed. Do not reuse real passwords or enter sensitive information.

Model scores and alerts are experimental signals, can be wrong, and are not evidence of misconduct. A qualified human must independently review any result before it is used.

Privacy Notice

For local demo accounts, the browser stores the name, email address, and a client-side SHA-256 password digest in local storage. A small session marker is kept in local storage when “Remember this device” is selected, or in session storage otherwise. These mechanisms are user-controlled and are not secure authentication.

The page loads typefaces from Google Fonts, so a browser may make requests to Google to fetch those assets. Dashboard examples are synthetic fixtures. This prototype does not provide a verified privacy-preserving federated-learning service, production bank integration, or a guarantee that submitted information is suitable for sensitive data.

Before production use, the project owner must identify the data controller, purposes and lawful basis, retention, recipients and subprocessors, user rights, contact details, and applicable regional notices. Ask the project owner how to request account deletion or data access.
